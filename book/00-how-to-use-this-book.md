# Chapter 0: How to use this book

## Who this book is for

- Graduates and career-changers starting their first **Business Analyst (BA)** job in an ICT company.
- Developers, testers or support staff moving into a BA role.
- Project Managers, Product Owners and team leads who want to understand what their BA should (and should not) be doing.

You do not need a technical background to read it. Where technical ideas appear, they are explained in plain language.

## How the book is organised

| Part | What's inside | Read it when... |
|------|---------------|-----------------|
| **I. Foundations** | What a BA is, and the shape of an ICT project | First, before anything else |
| **II. Lifecycle** | One chapter per phase, from idea to closure | In order, like a story |
| **III. Roles** | The BA compared with PM, PO, architect, QA and others | When you're unsure "is this my job?" |
| **IV. Guides** | When a BA is needed, Agile vs Waterfall, your first 90 days, career | Whenever you need them |
| **V. Templates** | Ready-to-copy documents | When you need to produce something |

## How each lifecycle chapter is built

Every chapter in Part II has the same sections, so you always know where to look:

| Section | Tells you |
|---------|-----------|
| **What's happening** | The state of the project in this phase |
| **Why the BA matters here** | What goes wrong if the BA is absent |
| **Your responsibilities** | A checklist of what you actually do |
| **Who you work with** | The people and what you need from each other |
| **Techniques** | The tools of the trade for this phase |
| **Deliverables** | What you produce, with links to templates |
| **QuickClaim in this phase** | The running example, so you see it in action |
| **Common mistakes** | Traps new BAs fall into |
| **Exit checklist** | How you know this phase is done |

## A note on methodology

ICT companies deliver projects in different ways: **Waterfall** (phase by phase), **Agile/Scrum** (short iterations), or a **hybrid** of both. This book walks through the phases in order because it is the clearest way to learn. In Agile projects the same activities still happen, but they repeat in small cycles instead of happening once. See [The BA in Agile vs Waterfall](../guides/ba-in-agile-vs-waterfall.md) for how it maps.

## A note on terminology

Job titles vary between companies. You may be called a *Business Analyst, Business Systems Analyst, Functional Consultant, Requirements Engineer* or *Product Analyst*. The work described here is the same. When in doubt, check the [Glossary](../glossary.md).
