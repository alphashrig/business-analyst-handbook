# The Business Analyst Handbook

### From the first idea to project closure: a practical guide for new Business Analysts in ICT companies

---

You've just joined an ICT (Information and Communication Technology) company as a Business Analyst. People keep asking for "the requirements", the Project Manager wants a timeline, developers want "clear stories", and the client keeps changing their mind.

This handbook is written for you.

Read it like a book, front to back. It follows a single ICT project from the moment someone has an **idea** to the day the project is formally **closed**, and at every stage it answers four questions:

1. **What is happening** in the project at this stage?
2. **What does the Business Analyst do** here, and why does it matter?
3. **Who do you work with**, and where does your job end and theirs begin?
4. **What do you produce**, and what should it look like?

## The story we follow

To keep things concrete, every chapter follows one fictional project:

> **"QuickClaim"**: *BrightCover Insurance* wants customers to file motor insurance claims from a mobile app instead of calling the contact centre. Your company, an ICT services firm, has been asked to help. You are the Business Analyst assigned to the account.

You'll see QuickClaim go from a one-line idea in a sales meeting to a live app, and finally to a closed project with measured benefits.
